# Home screen

The one screen the user sees: the ports as cards, and the focused port's details and actions. Everything works with a gamepad alone (`PROD-7`).

## Implementation notes

- Screens are `Control` scenes under `app/scenes/` with scripts in `app/src/ui/`, built from the `Theme` (`LOOK-2`). Colours and sizes come from the theme, never from literals in scenes.
- The screen talks only to `Ports` (`PORT-14`). Tests and screenshots use `ports_fake.gd`, which has fixed demo data, including a state of every kind.
- Focus uses Godot's `Control` focus and the `ui_*` actions, so remapped bindings work.
- `info(id).error` is shown with its first line as the title (`Could not install “Ship of Harkinian”`) and the lines after it as the detail.
- Without cover art, a card shows a generated emblem: the game's short name built from its title (the part after a colon; initials, lowercase for lowercase words, numbers whole: `OoT`, `MM`, `SF64`, `MK64`, `SM64`) on a tile in one of the palette's accent colours, chosen by catalog position. A catalog `monogram` replaces the generated name where the community's short name is not the initials (`PM64` for Paper Mario, `BK` for Banjo-Kazooie); `info.monogram` carries it, `""` when the catalog has none.
- The cards row sits in a `ScrollContainer` with its scroll bar hidden; the screen scrolls it by hand when focus moves (not `follow_focus`, which would leave no part of the next card showing).
- The app menu and the Quit question are about no card, so no card keeps the selection look while they are open. `menu` closes the app menu as well as back does.
- The hint bar's hints can be clicked (or tapped on the Deck's screen): a click sends the hint's action, which is how the mouse reaches the app menu (`HOME-14`). Back at the top is not hinted: Quit is in the app menu, and the bar has to fit 1280 px beside `Choose ROM folder` (`test_home_row::test_the_hint_bar_fits_in_every_state`).
- Focus and selection look different: the focused card has an accent ring; the card a menu or dialog is about keeps an accent-tinted fill while focus is in the menu.
- `main.gd` uses `res://src/ports.gd` when it exists and `ports_fake.gd` otherwise. For screenshots and UI checks, `--fake-ports [SCENARIO]` after `--` forces the fake (`anchorage -- --fake-ports`, `godot --path app -- --fake-ports offline`); the scenarios are listed in `ports_fake.gd`.
